My Shrinking Header
This tagline hides — header slims down — when you scroll

Scroll down 👇

Header sticks to the top and shrinks to a slim bar. Scroll back up and it grows again. Works on iOS Safari because it animates max-height, not height.

What changed vs. v1

v1 animated height — that re-lays-out every frame and breaks on mobile Safari. v2 animates max-height with overflow:hidden, which the browser can composite smoothly.

Root-cause check

No height:100%, no flex wrapper, no overflow-x on any header ancestor — sticky fires reliably.
